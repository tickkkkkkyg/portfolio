What I treat, and what it involves

Every appointment starts with an examination and a written estimate. Nothing below happens without you agreeing to it first.

01

Check-ups and professional cleaning

Examination of gums, bite and decay, then a scale and polish. Usually 30 to 40 minutes. Recommended twice a year, more often with gum history.

02

Toothache and dental infection

Same-day slots for acute pain. Antibiotics only when a spreading infection calls for them, not for a tooth that needs draining. Relief comes first, repair follows.

03

Fillings

Composite resin bonded to the tooth, in a shade close to your own. Placed under rubber dam so the surface stays dry and the filling lasts longer.

04

Root canal treatment

Removal of infected nerve tissue, cleaning and shaping the canals, then sealing. One or two visits. A crown is usually recommended afterwards to stop the tooth splitting.

05

Crowns, onlays and bridges

Ceramic or zirconia, bonded rather than drilled where possible. An onlay covers only the damaged part, which saves more of the tooth than a full crown.

06

Gum disease treatment

Scaling and root planing to remove deposits below the gumline, with a written plan for the home cleaning that keeps it from returning.

07

Extractions, including wisdom teeth

Simple and surgical removal, including referral for the cases that belong with an oral surgeon. The decision to remove a tooth is never made in a single unplanned visit.

08

Whitening

Take-home trays with a gel you wear over several nights. Slower than an in-chair session, gentler on enamel, and noticeably cheaper.

09

Veneers

Thin ceramic facings for a chipped edge, a discoloured tooth or a shape you dislike. I will show you a preview before anything is prepared, because this is largely irreversible.

10

Children's dentistry

Check-ups, fluoride varnish and fissure sealants. First visits are a ride in the chair and a count of the teeth, nothing more.
